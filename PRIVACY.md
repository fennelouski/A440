# A440 privacy policy

Updated September 27, 2026.

A440 plays bundled reference tones on your device. It does not require an account, access your microphone, collect personal information, include advertising or analytics, or send your activity to a server.

Your last instrument and design preference are stored locally on your device. Audio interruptions stop playback; the app does not record audio.

Privacy and support links open external websites, which have their own privacy policies. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to answer your request.

Contact: https://nathanfennel.com/contact
